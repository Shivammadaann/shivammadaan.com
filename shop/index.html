How do I purchase a song or its stems?

Each digital download and each project stems package costs ₹99. Choose Buy now next to your preferred format and complete payment in Razorpay’s secure checkout. Keep the payment ID shown after your payment is verified.

Which file formats and stems are included?

Available audio formats and the exact contents of a stems package are confirmed for your chosen song before purchase. Mention any specific format or production requirements in your enquiry.

How will I receive my files?

Files are currently delivered manually. After your payment is verified, use Contact about this payment to arrange delivery directly with Shivam Madaan. There is no automatic download link. Contact the shop before buying if you need to confirm file contents or delivery timing.
